# CN / KR / TW / EN community research: SVWB Rotation, pack 9 (复苏的阿兹弗特), post-2026-09-29 patch
Collected 2026-10-07 with WebSearch/WebFetch. Tags: [V2] = two independent fetches or sources agree, or the text was fetched verbatim twice; [V1] = one source or one verbatim fetch; [UNVERIFIED] = from the summarizer or inferred.
Bilibili pages only render metadata (title, date, description, related titles). The tier rankings inside the videos could NOT be read, so there are no subtitles, chapters or comments.

## 0. Bottom line on China
- **No CN-server-specific meta difference was found.** Chinese content after the patch shows the same picture as JP: Pirate Royal buffed into T1, Highlander Nemesis buffed and rising, Ramp/"跳费" Dragon as a common opponent, Combo Elf dominant before the patch.
- Most Chinese deck content imports JP lists. Bilibili creators credit JP deck authors (Arusu, そろばん) and convert them to "国服长码" (CN-server deck codes). yokidou's 连胜卡组合集 is a Chinese translation of JP win-streak data.
- **CN pack timing is synced with JP** [V2]:
  - The official CN Bilibili account 影之诗调戏专员 wrote on 2026-08-24: "第九弹卡包"复苏的阿兹弗特"将在8月27日正式上线！" (BV13MhP6GEjK, description fetched verbatim twice).
  - The CN App Store notes tag pack and balance content 【同步】 (synced) and CN-only content 【国服】.
  - The CN app (网易移动游戏, id1297191124) version 11.9.5 is dated 9月16日 with the note "【同步】新卡包"复苏的阿兹弗特"上线". This is NOT a launch lag: App Store version dates trail content dates (the JP WB app lists pack 9 under 1.9.1 dated 9月1日 even though the pack launched 8/27).
- **CN received the same 9/29 buffs** [V2, indirect]. Bilibili titles:
  - "旗皇！大加强😈" (困倦keeexing, 2026-09-28)
  - "宇宙鱼质变加强！…新版本最高分构筑" (困倦keeexing, 2026-09-30)

  - "宇宙超越者，增强后问鼎天梯" (星野饼美, 2026-10-04)
  - yokidou BO10 (2026-10-07): "上次打还是55的，加强后效果拔群啊"
  - CN app 11.9.10 (9月28日) notes include card-ability changes [V1, from the summarizer].
- **The one real CN-only difference is a new mode, not a ladder difference.** 影之幻境 ("Shadow Mirage") is a CN-exclusive auto-battler (自走棋) mode. It has no bearing on the Rotation ladder meta.
  - Official intro video BV1uNeX6JEPY, uploaded 2026-09-19 by 影之诗调戏专员: "即将在9月底开启先锋删档测试" [V1 verbatim].

## 1. CN terminology (needed for any further searches)
- Class names [V2]: 超越者 = Nemesis (gamersky's 超越者卡组推荐 lists 人偶仇 and 造物仇). Community slang for Nemesis is 鱼 or 仇: 造物鱼, 人偶鱼, 奥义鱼, 宇宙鱼. Royal = 皇 or 皇家; Elf = 妖 or 妖精; Witch = 法 or 巫师; Dragon = 龙; Nightmare = 梦 or 梦魇; Bishop = 教 or 主教.
- Rotation is called 指定模式 (tag on AKNO_'s video); Unlimited is 无限制模式.
- Deck names, mapped to JP [V1, from yokidou's list and video titles]:
  - 海贼皇 / 海盗旗皇 / 旗皇 = 海賊ロイヤル
  - 连携皇 = 連携ロイヤル
  - 连击妖 = コンボエルフ
  - 中速妖
  - 魔手法 = 魔手ウィッチ
  - 增幅法 = スペルウィッチ
  - 实验体巫师 = 実験体ウィッチ
  - 跳费龙 = ランプドラゴン
  - 打脸龙 = フェイスドラゴン
  - 中速梦 = ミッドレンジナイトメア
  - 快攻梦 = アグロナイトメア
  - 护符教 = アミュレットビショップ
  - 骰子教
  - 造物鱼 = AFネメシス
  - 奥义鱼
  - 人偶鱼
  - 宇宙鱼 / 宇宙超越者 = ハイランダーネメシス. "宇宙" is the Chinese term for highlander; this mapping is inferred from the 9/29 buffs to ハクガ燐斂 and 屈辱なる放逐 [UNVERIFIED].
- Cards: 巴巴洛丝 = バルバロス ("出航的罪人·巴巴洛丝"); 卡特斯罗特 does not appear in Chinese titles found. KR community calls Cutthroat Nemesis "컷스로트 네메".
