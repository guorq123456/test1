# SNC 2026 Grand Finals: decklists (researched 2026-10-07)

Event: Shadowverse NetEase Championship 2026 年度总决赛, 2026-09-19, NetEase HQ, Guangzhou. 8 players, 2-deck BO3, single elimination (quarterfinals, semifinals, 3rd-place match, final).

**Result: I found official deck codes for 1 of the 8 players (the champion).**
- No public source I could reach has the other 7 players' lists or codes.
- The only known aggregator, svlabo, publishes them as an image and as JavaScript-rendered content. Neither was readable from here (see "Not found").

## Comments from players and casters
**莉莉猪** (beyond-dexel interview, Japanese translation):
- On her hands: "試合が終わった後は手が震えていました"
- Key game (paraphrase): after two poor Abyss (天晶深渊) rolls she had run out of damage and "had already resigned", then drew her last 卡卢基典瑟拉 and found lethal for the next turn. [paraphrase via summarizer]
- On her twin 铃铃猪, who lost in QF to 离场王: she said she wanted his conqueror to keep winning "so that in the final I can avenge my brother". She then beat 离场王 in the final.

**Clip title**, BV1Txeb6mEp8 (迫进的暗影, 2026-09-19, SF 莉莉猪 vs 骇鳞): "莉莉猪在手出出了的情况下，靠卡场1血翻盘". She won at 1 defense.
