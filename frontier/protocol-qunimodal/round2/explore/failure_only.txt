===== R2E1 (outcome FAILED, candidates tried 23)
S2 is not fully proved. (i) is proved, and so is every part of (ii) except one inequality.

What remains: for b in U with b != F (mod 2) and b>=4, show b-3 is in U. Equivalently, show B_off >= B*-3, where B_off is the largest element of U with parity F.

How it reduces: by the thread criterion (Theorem 12), this is a statement about each thread separately. For j == F (mod 2) with V_j<0, it requires V_j < d_j - d_{j+1} - d_{j+3}. It would follow from inequality L': d_j - d_{j+1} >= d_{j+3} at the first off-parity failure. In positions this reads delta_u - delta_v >= delta_{v-r}, where v<u<v+r are consecutive thread positions.

Why the proved lemmas are not enough: unimodality of delta plus the sign lemma do not imply (C). The abstract sequence d=(2,1,1,1,1,1) with F even satisfies both lemmas but violates (C). Over all abstract unimodal sequences of length up to 7 with entries up to 4, 82 of 8659 violate it (abstract_thread.py). A proof therefore needs quantitative arithmetic of (1-q)prod[a_i]_q: an off-parity failure must never happen at a level where delta grows slowly.

How big the gap is: a pure exponential model delta=e^{lambda y}, with thread gaps g and r-g, has a violation window exactly when e^{-lambda g}(1+e^{-lambda r})>1, roughly lambda<1/(r+2g). No instance in the fit box comes close:
- L' held at all 3270 high failures checked, with minimum ratio 1.67e5.
- Inside the fit box (Theorem 17 coverage), failures fall at the very bottom of the thread in all 100488 exhaustive sets and in 1277 of 1500 random ones.
- Large-k instances can fail high (height 9 for r=30, a=[15]^40), but there delta grows very fast.

Evidence for S2 itself: 0 violations in 100488 exhaustive and 12000 random sets.

===== R2E2 (outcome PROOF, candidates tried 16)
This is a partial proof, not a proof of S2 for all parameters. The peeling path proves that S2 follows from a residue-level certificate: three finite statements (TP_rho, E_rho, O_rho) about the small-part instance rho, with all parts below r. Lifting (Theorem B) shows that the part sizes never matter. What is not done is a proof of that certificate for every rho; the attempt to prove it by adding one small part at a time did not close. Adding a part [s]_q convolves d, tau and Q with s ones. That turns ND(K') into a mix of pair conditions at the sums K'-2i for i<s, and these fall in both residue classes of K mod 2r. Only one class (E) holds for every K; the other (O) holds only above 2mu*+3r. So a single-part induction loses the parity bookkeeping. The certificate holds for all 3,154,273 rho swept (0 failures). The k-independent strong version, which covers any number of parts that are 1 mod r, fails for 25,737 of 1,547,599 long rho', so it cannot serve as the universal residue statement. Every failure has many parts: there are none with k'<=5 for r<=12 or k'<=3 for r<=30. Proving TP/E/O for all rho, for example from cyclic unimodality of prod[rho_i] mod (q^r-1) (0 failures in test_cyclic_unimodal.py, unproved), remains open.

===== R2E3 (outcome FAILED, candidates tried 26)
S2 is only partly proved. The part still open is the odd shift b -> b-3 in S2(ii).

What is proved (see proof_S2_partial.txt):
- U = [1,sigma] u {sigma+2, sigma+4, ..., tau*-2}, with sigma and tau* both congruent to 1+F mod 2 and tau* >= sigma+2.
- From this: S2(i) holds; max U is congruent to 1+F mod 2; U is closed under b -> b-2; and S2 is equivalent to tau* <= sigma+4.

How the proof goes:
- Two pairing arguments on the centred difference sequence e: Lemma 3 shows e is unimodal on the positive half; Lemma 4 is the exact zigzag criterion over class pairs +-delta.
- Lemma 7 (new) shows the class sums of prod[a_i] are cyclically unimodal, peaked at S/2. Its proof uses an arc-overlap lemma on Z_r. Lemma 8 turns this into the sign rule that fixes all parities.

Where it breaks: for each class pair delta, the shape of U_delta needs (Z2): A_{s0+2} + E_{s0+4} < 0, with s0 the first index where the alternating sum A_s goes negative.
- The paired identity Q_{s0} = E_{s0} + A_{s0-2} >= 0 only gives t1 >= s0+2. The needed bound t1 <= s0+4 cannot come from pairing alone.
- e-unimodality plus cyclic unimodality are not enough for the S2 shape. They fail on 102/2312 generic symmetric A, and on 226/10859 generic log-concave A (generic_cu.py, generic_cu_lc.py). So any proof of (Z2) must use quantitative product structure: it has to compare the class sum |tilde-tau(delta)| with the e-samples near the end of the support.
- The easy route s0 >= L-3 (which would make E_{s0+4}=0) is false. In the large-k stress set it fails in 495/1944 cases, with s0-L as low as -8.
- The robust inequality E_{s0+1} >= E_{s0+2} + E_{s0+4} holds at s0 in 1847/1847 cases. But it fails as a uniform lemma: its validity threshold sbar lies up to 28 steps past the E-peak. It would therefore need a separate proof that s0 >= sbar, which is no easier.

Empirically (Z2) holds everywhere tested: 0 failures in 5552 + 1944 class-pair instances. Also tau*-sigma is always 2 or 4 on 20233 instances, and s_neg - s0 is always 0 or 2 on 1705 instances. A proof of (Z2) would finish S2 with no other changes.
